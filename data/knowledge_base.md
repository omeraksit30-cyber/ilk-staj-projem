# Demo Restaurant Group — Fictional Portfolio Knowledge Base

All content below is synthetic and fictional. It is intended only for an educational
portfolio demonstration and must not be treated as real operational guidance.

## Opening Hours

Demo Restaurant Group dining rooms are described in this fictional example as open
from 11:00 to 21:00 Monday through Thursday and from 11:00 to 22:00 Friday through
Sunday. Holiday schedules are not included. Guests should use the fictional support
contact process for questions not covered here.

## Allergen Information Process

Guests may ask a team member for the current fictional ingredient and allergen guide
before ordering. The guide is informational and does not guarantee an allergen-free
environment because shared preparation areas are part of this synthetic scenario.
Medical decisions should be discussed with a qualified professional.

## Reservation Policy

Reservations in this fictional example may be requested for groups of up to eight.
Requests can be changed or cancelled through the same fictional booking channel.
A request is not confirmed until the guest receives a confirmation message.
Walk-in seating depends on availability.

## Sprout Points Loyalty Program

Sprout Points is a fictional loyalty program used for this portfolio. Members receive
one point for each whole fictional currency unit spent on eligible menu items.
Rewards and eligibility are shown in the fictional booking profile; no real monetary
value, campaign, or financial account is represented.

## Delivery Areas

Delivery availability is determined by the postal-zone selector in the fictional
ordering experience. Areas and estimated times can change based on capacity. This
knowledge base intentionally contains no real addresses or operational delivery map.

## Customer Feedback

Guests can submit feedback through the fictional support form in their order summary.
They should include a general description and avoid personal, payment, health, or
credential information. This demonstration does not persist feedback submissions.

## Menu Categories

The fictional menu uses four broad categories: Garden Plates, Hearth Bowls, Small
Bakes, and Seasonal Sips. These category names describe example food and beverage
groups only. Availability, ingredients, and prices are not supplied by this portfolio.

## Support Contact Process

For questions not answered by the fictional knowledge base, guests are directed to
the support form in the demonstration order summary. The portfolio contains no real
contact address or phone number. Submitting a form is outside this application's scope.
